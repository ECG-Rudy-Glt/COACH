# Code of Conduct

We follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

**Our pledge.** Participation in this project is harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

**Expected:** be respectful and constructive; give and accept feedback graciously; focus on what is best for the project and its users; assume good faith.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, personal attacks, sexualised language or imagery, doxxing, publishing others' private information (including their health data), or other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting. Because this project handles health information, **never post real personal health data in issues or PRs** — use synthetic examples.

**Enforcement.** Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits and issues that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors. Report concerns privately through GitHub (a maintainer's profile or the repository's *Security → Report a vulnerability* form for sensitive matters). Reports are handled confidentially.
